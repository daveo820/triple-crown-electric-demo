# Design DNA: Triple Crown Electric concept

- **Archetype:** asymmetric hero. Headline left, the "panel schedule" card right, dropping 136px into the navy meter band below it (the home page grid break). Not used in any earlier LuminArch demo.
- **Type:** Archivo at 112 to 125% width, weight 800 to 900, for display. Public Sans for body (17px, line height 1.65). Martian Mono for numbers, labels and circuit tags. None of these were used in earlier demos.
- **Color story (from the Triple Crown logo):** crown `#1c2a63` (navy bands, headings), sovereign `#3a4ca3` (logo wordmark blue, accents), gilt `#d9ae3c` (logo bolt, dark backgrounds and fills only), gilt dk `#8a6a12`, paper `#f5f3ee` (panel door enamel), steel `#d7dae2` (rules), ink `#15182a` (text), slate `#545a70` (muted), hot `#c2361b` (focus ring and signal only, like a hot wire).
- **Signature element:** the circuit tag. A tiny breaker toggle plus "CKT 01" in mono. Used in every kicker, the panel schedule, the photo captions, the footer ("CKT 20 Main breaker") and the 404. The panel directory label card in the footer is its big version.
- **Motion personality:** breaker snap. Blocks rise 18px in 0.45s on a hard ease out, and the toggles in each block flip from off to on as it arrives. On the home page the twelve panel breakers flip on one by one. All of it is off under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Grid breaks:** home, panel card overlapping the meter band. Record, the two chart cards ride up into the navy page head. Services, one bordered two column "panel" with the commercial side in navy.
- **Footer:** closing spread styled as a panel door. A gold inset border, "One call." plus the phone number at display size, and a tilted panel directory card with owner, shop, license, email and rating.
- **Distinct from earlier demos:** light background where competitors go dark; no full bleed photo (Hollingsworth), no scorecard collage (Edgewood), no editorial split (Core Home), no type only hero (Eagle Point).
